Independent Study

An independent research project focused on developing an automated image-processing pipeline in MATLAB to identify structural micro-cracks in wind turbine blades. Developed to analyze high-resolution surface images captured by an automated ground-based inspection cart during post-dismount maintenance. The algorithm leverages MATLAB datastores to sequentially batch-process entire image directories, applying Hessian-based filters to isolate linear crack features and utilizing morphological dilation and erosion to filter out surface noise and highlight defect boundaries.





Prosthetic Hand

Developed with SolidWorks as a specialized project for a university additive manufacturing course, this multi-material prosthetic hand explores the limits of multi-part functional assemblies. The model features 16 individual joints designed with functional spaces as small as 0.01 inches to allow for free rotation, utilizing a strategic combination of FDM, SLA, and PolyJet printing to seamlessly integrate rigid structures with flexible components.
